About this piece

James Lord Pierpont wrote this song, and it was published in 1857 under the title “One Horse Open Sleigh”.

Today it is one of the best-loved Christmas songs. The chorus starts with “E E E, E E E”, so you can play it almost right away.

How to play it in Plush Keys

  1. Open the app and tap “Play a Song”.
  2. Type “Jingle Bells” in the search box and pick the song.
  3. When a falling colored bar reaches the line, press the key of the same color. If it feels hard, choose “Easy”.

“E E E”, “E E E”, then “E G C D E”. Keep it bouncy, like ringing sleigh bells.

A free song. The longer version is free, too.

Questions

Can I play it without reading sheet music?

Yes. In Plush Keys, colored bars fall from the top. When a bar reaches the line, press the key of the same color. You don't need to know note names or read music. When you're ready, you can switch to a staff view (Premium).

Is it free to play?

Yes. “Jingle Bells” is a free song: download the app and play it right away. The longer version is free, too.

Can I use a real piano or keyboard?

Yes. Play on the on-screen keyboard of your iPhone or iPad, or connect a USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard or digital piano and follow the colors on real keys.

No sheet music needed: follow the colors and play “Jingle Bells”.
