About this piece

“Frère Jacques” is a traditional French song and one of the best-known rounds, where singers start the same tune one after another. In English it is sung as “Are You Sleeping, Brother John?”

Gustav Mahler turned the melody into a minor key for the third movement of his Symphony No. 1.

How to play it in Plush Keys

  1. Open the app and tap “Play a Song”.
  2. Type “Frere” in the search box and pick the song.
  3. When a falling colored bar reaches the line, press the key of the same color. If it feels hard, choose “Easy”.

Every phrase is played twice. Play the last “C G C” like ringing bells.
