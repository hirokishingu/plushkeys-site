About this piece

The French composer Erik Satie wrote his three Gymnopédies in 1888. The first, with its slow three-beat sway and calm, mysterious harmonies, is the best known.

The original is in D major; the Plush Keys arrangement is moved so you can play it on white keys only.

How to play it in Plush Keys

  1. Open the app and tap “Play a Song”.
  2. Type “Gymnopedie” in the search box and pick the song.
  3. When a falling colored bar reaches the line, press the key of the same color. If it feels hard, choose “Easy”.

This is a very slow piece. Hold each note a little longer and press the keys gently.

A Premium song (one-time purchase, $3.99 in the US, with a 3-day free trial).

Questions

Can I play it without reading sheet music?

Yes. In Plush Keys, colored bars fall from the top. When a bar reaches the line, press the key of the same color. You don't need to know note names or read music. When you're ready, you can switch to a staff view (Premium).

Is it free to play?

“Gymnopédie No. 1” is a Premium song. Premium is a one-time purchase ($3.99 in the US) with a 3-day free trial, and no subscription. Some days it is also picked as Today's Song, when one Premium song is free to play.

Can I use a real piano or keyboard?

Yes. Play on the on-screen keyboard of your iPhone or iPad, or connect a USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard or digital piano and follow the colors on real keys.

No sheet music needed: follow the colors and play “Gymnopédie No. 1”.
