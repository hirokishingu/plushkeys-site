About this piece

This minuet appears in the Notebook for Anna Magdalena Bach (1725). For a long time it was thought to be by Johann Sebastian Bach, but today it is credited to Christian Petzold.

It is a dance in three beats and a favorite at piano recitals.

How to play it in Plush Keys

  1. Open the app and tap “Play a Song”.
  2. Type “Minuet in G” in the search box and pick the song.
  3. When a falling colored bar reaches the line, press the key of the same color. If it feels hard, choose “Easy”.

The only black key is F♯. Hum the rhythm “D, G-A-B-C, D, G, G” as you play and it will stick quickly.
