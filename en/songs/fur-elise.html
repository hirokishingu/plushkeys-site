About this piece

Beethoven wrote this short piano piece (WoO 59) around 1810. It was published in 1867, forty years after his death, and became one of the best-known piano pieces in the world.

Nobody knows for certain who “Elise” was. The opening, rocking back and forth between E and D♯, is a melody almost everyone has heard.

How to play it in Plush Keys

  1. Open the app and tap “Play a Song”.
  2. Type “Fur Elise” in the search box and pick the song.
  3. When a falling colored bar reaches the line, press the key of the same color. If it feels hard, choose “Easy”.

The opening “E D♯ E D♯ E” just moves between a white key and the black key right next to it. Repeat it a few times and your fingers will remember it.

Questions

Can I play it without reading sheet music?

Yes. In Plush Keys, colored bars fall from the top. When a bar reaches the line, press the key of the same color. You don't need to know note names or read music. When you're ready, you can switch to a staff view (Premium).

Is it free to play?

Yes. “Für Elise” is a free song: download the app and play it right away. The longer version is part of Premium.

Can I use a real piano or keyboard?

Yes. Play on the on-screen keyboard of your iPhone or iPad, or connect a USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard or digital piano and follow the colors on real keys.
